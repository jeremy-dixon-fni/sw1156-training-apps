CHANNEL INVESTMENT & ANNUALIZED LOSS EXPLORER — PROTOTYPE

Run it:
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. No Python, server, install, or internet connection is required.

Purpose:
This is an illustrative teaching prototype. It uses synthetic flows, simplified Manning calculations,
a conceptual fixed-invert compound section, fixed synthetic structure rows, a simple depth-damage curve,
and an earthwork cost proxy. Alternative sections hold the existing channel invert in place while excavating
1% floodplain benches with 3H:1V cut faces. It is not a design-level hydraulic or economic model.
Structure-ground presets project the synthetic structure rows outward from the floodplain tie-ins at 0.5%,
1%, or 2%; the selected preset updates both the section drawing and the associated damages.

Suggested teaching sequence:
- Click "Try a tiny change" and save it.
- Click "Try ~100-yr" and save it.
- Build and save 2-, 5-, 10-, 25-, and 50-ish capacity alternatives.
- Watch the cost-benefit curve fill in and compare it with the direct event-damage bars.

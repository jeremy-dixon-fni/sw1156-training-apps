Library distributions are read from CSV files listed in data/temporal_distributions/manifest.json. The dropdown label is the CSV filename without .csv.

The processed storm uses the entered 100-year 24-hour depth when provided; otherwise it uses the Atlas 14 100-year 24-hour depth.

Assumptions

  • Atlas input is a PDS precipitation depth CSV in inches.
  • The reference event is fixed at the 100-year ARI.
  • The generated storm duration is 24 hours.
  • The generated storm timestep is 5 minutes.
  • Library distributions use unitless cumulative columns: fraction_time and fraction_cumulative_depth.
  • Atlas durations longer than 2 days are excluded.
  • IDF comparison uses rolling maximum depths from the processed storm.
  • Durations longer than 24 hours include zero rainfall after the storm ends.

Method notes and limitations

Atlas 14 depths are parsed by duration and recurrence interval. Durations longer than 2 days are excluded. Intensities are calculated as depth divided by duration.

Library temporal distributions are fractional time and fractional cumulative depth CSVs. The app interpolates each cumulative curve to 5-minute intervals and scales it to the selected 24-hour total.

Alternating Block uses the 100-year Atlas 14 depth-duration curve through 24 hours, interpolates intermediate durations on log-log axes, and rearranges incremental depths around the selected center.

The processed-storm IDF curve is a rolling-maximum diagnostic that shows whether a temporal distribution reproduces, exceeds, or falls below Atlas 14 at each duration.

Rules represented

  • The storm is fixed at 10.0 inches over 24 hours.
  • Impervious rainfall bypasses losses and becomes direct runoff.
  • Initial loss is a basin-average depth applied only against the pervious rainfall volume.
  • After initial loss is satisfied, constant loss is applied to the remaining pervious rainfall.
  • Any pervious rainfall left after those losses becomes runoff.

Method details

For each time step, rainfall is split into impervious and pervious components.

Impervious runoff equals rainfall multiplied by percent impervious. This portion does not see either the initial loss or the constant loss rate.

Pervious rainfall first fills the remaining basin-average initial loss. Once that abstraction is satisfied, the constant loss rate is converted to a time-step capacity and subtracted from the remaining pervious rainfall.

The model enforces mass balance at every time step: rainfall equals runoff plus initial loss plus constant loss.

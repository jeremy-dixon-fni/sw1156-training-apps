Fixed teaching assumptions
  • 2-, 5-, 10-, 25-, 50-, and 100-year synthetic flows
  • Fixed existing channel invert and 20-ft bottom; 1% benches with 3H:1V channel and cut side slopes
  • Structure-ground presets project outward from the tie-ins at 0.5%, 1%, or 2%
  • Manning n = 0.040 channel / 0.085 overbank; bed slope = 0.0015 ft/ft
  • 2,500-ft nominal project length; $40/cy excavation + 20% indirects
  • Structures valued at $500,000; depth-damage capped at 50%
  • 50-year analysis period, 3% discount rate
